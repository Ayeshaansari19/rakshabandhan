# 🎀 Rakshabandhan — A Little Surprise for My Brother ❤️

A cute, interactive **Rakshabandhan surprise website** made especially for my brother. 🫶🏻

This website is designed as a small journey through three interactive pages — starting with a playful question, moving through personalized cards and messages, and ending with a heartfelt letter.

## 🌸 Live Website

### 👉 [💗 Open the Rakshabandhan Surprise](https://ayeshaansari19.github.io/rakshabandhan/)

---

## ✨ What's Inside?

### 💕 Page 1 — The Beginning

A cute **Happy Rakshabandhan** welcome page featuring:

* 🎀 Rakshabandhan greeting
* 📸 Personal photo gallery
* ✨ Hover animations on photos
* 💗 "Do you love your sister?" question
* 😈 A mischievous **NO** button that runs away
* 🥹 YES button to continue to the next page

---

### 💌 Page 2 — Little Things From Your Sister

An interactive collection of personalized cards.

Each card flips when you hover over it and reveals a different message.

Cards include:

* 🔮 A Little Reading
* 💭 Things To Remember
* 🌱 Your Future
* 🫂 When Life Gets Hard
* 🚨 Emergency Sister Support
* 🧠 Stop Overthinking
* ⭐ Something I See In You
* 🥹 From Your Sister
* 🎁 Things You Deserve
* 🔮 A Tiny Prediction
* 🤫 Secret Message
* 🧿 A Little Protection
* 🏆 When You Doubt Yourself
* 💀 Brother.exe
* ❤️ One Last Thing

And of course...

> **"Have you prepared my Rakhi gift?" 🎁**

The **NO** button isn't getting away that easily. 😭

---

### ❤️ Page 3 — The Final Letter

The final page contains a personal **Hinglish letter** filled with:

* 🫶🏻 Love
* 🧿 Blessings
* 🌸 Good wishes
* 🌟 Encouragement
* 💭 Life advice
* ❤️ A message from sister to brother

And obviously...

> **"Rakhi ke saath treat bhi deni padegi." 😌😂**

```bash

```

Open the project folder:

```bash
cd rakshabandhan
```

Then simply open:

```text
index.html
```

in your browser.
